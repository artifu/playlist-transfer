About

Move Spotify playlists to Apple Music with the matches visible.

PlaylistXfer turns a public Spotify playlist or song link into a new Apple Music playlist. Before anything is created, you can see which songs matched, review uncertain results, and leave out anything that looks wrong.

What PlaylistXfer does

  • Reads public Spotify playlist and song links without asking for a Spotify login.
  • Finds Apple Music matches and lets you review uncertain songs.
  • Creates a new playlist after you connect Apple Music.

Spotify and Apple Music have different catalogs, so local files, unavailable releases, and some alternate versions may not have an exact match. PlaylistXfer shows those gaps instead of silently replacing a song.

Privacy

Apple Music access is requested only when you create the destination playlist. PlaylistXfer does not need your Spotify password and never modifies the source playlist. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Independent project

PlaylistXfer is not affiliated with Spotify or Apple. You can report a problem through support or inspect the project on GitHub.
